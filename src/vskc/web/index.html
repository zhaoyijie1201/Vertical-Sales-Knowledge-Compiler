        What it does

        An FDE configuring a sales agent for an industrial sensor supplier describes a customer situation. The system retrieves the relevant product records, sales policies and practice from a knowledge base, and a model recommends one next-best action from a closed set of nine, with a rationale, the evidence it relied on and a confidence score. A deterministic gate sends uncertain or poorly covered cases to a person.

        1. Scenarionarrative and structured fields
        2. RetrieveBM25, two queries fused, top 5
        3. Recommendone model call, JSON schema output
        4. Validateaction in the set, evidence was retrieved
        5. Gatepass, or human review with a reason

        Intended use and limits

        Intended use. Decision support for FDEs and sales implementation teams.

        Not for. Contacting customers, negotiating prices, making binding commitments, or replacing human approval of strategic decisions. Nothing here writes to any external system.

        • All data is synthetic. Results describe behaviour on this data, not on a live account.
        • Labels reflect one reviewer's judgement. Many held-out cases have a defensible alternative.
        • Confidence thresholds chosen on the dev set did not transfer to the held-out set.
        • The silent failure to watch for: a recommendation that reads as plausible and is wrong for the industry.
